    Good questions.

    Ask us on a call
    What's the free AI Ops Teardown?

    A free 30-minute call where we map one of your workflows live. You leave with a one-page plan showing what to automate first and its estimated return. If you want the full picture after that, the paid AI Ops Audit maps the whole business, and that's where builds get scoped.

    Why not just buy Claude or hire someone?

    You can, and the tool will work fine. What's usually missing is the system around it: mapped processes, clear SOPs, the right priorities, and guardrails. Without that, a subscription becomes shelfware and a new hire inherits the mess. We do the operator work first, then build.

    Will the AI send emails or make changes without me?

    No. Inbox and EA systems are drafts-only: nothing goes out until a person approves it. Financial write-backs (like QuickBooks) run with review steps and a freeze switch.

    What does “connect every system” actually mean?

    Email, CRM, accounting (QuickBooks or Xero), spreadsheets and your ops tools feed one place. Your dashboard and your AI employee both work from the same live data.

    Grok Bot or Claude Cowork?

    Whichever fits your tools and team. We set up either, and we'll recommend one on the call.

    Do I need to be technical?

    No. If you can use email and a spreadsheet, you can run this. Training is part of every build.

    Is my data safe?

    Your accounts stay yours and are connected with your own logins. We don't resell or share your data. See the privacy policy.

    What does it cost?

    The Teardown is free. Builds are one setup fee, scoped and fixed after the paid AI Ops Audit, plus whatever the software costs to run. No retainer trap. The only public price is the NJ Setup Day: $500 early-bird, then $750 per seat.

    What return should I expect?

    Every engagement is scoped to return 10–30x the setup fee, and the highest-impact builds go first. That's a target, not a guarantee. Results vary by business.

    Do I own what you build?

    Yes. The infrastructure runs on your accounts and your logins: every system, file and pipeline is yours long-term.

    What kinds of businesses do you work with?

    Small and growing teams: wholesale and distribution, e-com brands, agencies, real estate and property management, home services and contractors.
